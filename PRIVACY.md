# QuickFill Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

QuickFill is a free, open-source Chrome extension that keeps your personal details, Gmail account shortcuts, class schedule image, todos and notes one click away.

## Summary

QuickFill does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

## What QuickFill stores

You choose what to save. This can include your name, email address, phone number, postal address, other details you add yourself, your Gmail account labels and numbers, a schedule image, todos, notes, and your appearance settings (accent colour and Light or Dark mode).

All of this is saved locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party. QuickFill has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests of its own.

## How the data is used

- When you click a detail, it is copied to your clipboard.
- When you press **Insert**, QuickFill writes the chosen value into the form field you selected on the current page. This happens only when you press the button.
- When you click a Gmail account in the Email tab, QuickFill opens a `mail.google.com` link in a new tab. It does not read your Gmail or your Google login.

## Permissions

- `storage` and `unlimitedStorage`: save your data locally on your device. Unlimited storage is needed because a schedule image can be several megabytes.
- `activeTab` and `scripting`: used only when you press Insert, to fill the form field you selected on the current tab.

QuickFill requests no access to websites in advance, loads no remote code and uses no third-party libraries.

## Your control

You can edit or delete any information in the extension at any time. Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data from your browser.

## Things to be aware of

Data is stored as plain text in your Chrome profile and is not encrypted. Please do not store passwords, card numbers, bank details, or national ID or passport numbers in QuickFill. Websites can read what is inserted into their form fields, and your clipboard may be visible to other software on your computer.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new date.
